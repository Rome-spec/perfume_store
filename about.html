About Us

Our story, brewed in petals

Our Tale

Lily’s Floral Apothecary opened its doors in 2025 with a simple wish: to bring the charm of an old-world apothecary to modern perfume lovers. Every scent is chosen with care, from soft florals and warm woods to glowing ambers, bright citrus and sweet gourmands.

Today, a circle of eight dedicated apothecaries serves our community from Four Way Mall. We have welcomed 210 customers, and 72% of them return, which is the finest compliment a perfumer can receive.
